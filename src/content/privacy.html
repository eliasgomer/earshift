2. The apps (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Earshift processes the following information only locally on your device. It is never sent to me or to third parties:

  • Bluetooth. Earshift connects directly to your headphones over Bluetooth to control listening modes, equalizer and spatial audio and to read the battery level. Your device asks for your permission first.
  • Settings. Profiles, automations, shortcuts, widget settings and the last known headphone status are stored in the app's local storage and in its shared App Group storage, so widgets and Control Center can show them.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Focus filters. When you use Earshift actions through Siri, the Shortcuts app or Focus filters, Apple processes your input under Apple's privacy policy. Earshift only receives the chosen action (e.g. "listening mode Quiet").
  • Notifications and Live Activities. Alerts (e.g. low battery, end of a focus session) are created locally on your device. No push service is used.
  • Music playback. On iPhone and iPad, with your permission, Earshift shows what's playing in Apple Music and controls playback. On the Mac, with macOS's permission, Earshift can query and control Spotify or Apple Music on your Mac.
  • Suggestions (Mac). To suggest automations, Earshift counts locally which mode you pick while a given app is in front. These counts never leave your Mac and are removed when you dismiss a suggestion.

Network access

The iPhone and iPad app makes no server connections of its own. The Mac app only loads album artwork when Spotify is your music source; these images are loaded from the URL Spotify provides (Spotify's servers), which, like any web request, sees your IP address. No other data about you is sent.

The "Support", "Privacy Policy" and "Legal Notice" links in the app open this website in your browser (see section 4).

3. App Store, trial and purchase

Downloads, the free trial and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Earshift only receives a signed confirmation from Apple whether and when the trial was started or the purchase was made, to unlock its features. I never see your payment details.

In App Store Connect I can see aggregated statistics provided by Apple (e.g. number of downloads, or crashes if you agreed to share them with developers). They do not identify you.
