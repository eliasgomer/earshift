Trial, purchase and restore

Earshift is free to download. Start the free 7-day trial in the app; afterwards, unlock lifetime access with a one-time purchase - no subscription, nothing is charged automatically. One purchase unlocks Earshift on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account, and can be shared with your family via Family Sharing.

On a new device or after reinstalling, use Restore Purchases (iPhone/iPad: Settings → About; Mac: the menu, right-click menu or Settings → About). Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

iPhone and iPad

The headphones won't connect

  • Connect the headphones to your iPhone or iPad in Settings → Bluetooth first, then tap Connect in Earshift.
  • Allow Bluetooth for Earshift: Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
  • Close the Bose app - it can hold the control connection.

Widgets, Control Center and Action Button

Add the "Earshift" widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen and long-press it to choose buttons, content and design. Control Center and Action Button controls are listed under Earshift in the controls gallery. They switch modes in the background; the app doesn't need to be open. The in-app guide (Settings → Widgets & Shortcuts) shows every step.

The app shows the wrong mode

Button presses on the headphones are not reported over Bluetooth, so Earshift asks the headphones regularly while it is open. Pull down on the main screen for an instant update.

Mac

The headphones won't connect

  • Make sure the headphones are paired and connected to this Mac (System Settings → Bluetooth), then click Connect in the Earshift menu.
  • Allow Bluetooth access for Earshift: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
  • Close the Bose app on nearby phones or tablets - it can hold the control connection.

Now Playing shows nothing

Choose your music source in Settings → General and allow Earshift to control Spotify or Apple Music when macOS asks (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation).

Keyboard shortcuts

Set them in Settings → Shortcuts. They work system-wide and need no extra permissions. If a shortcut does nothing, another app may already use the same key combination.

Mode switching doesn't react

Tap or click Connect again. If it still doesn't respond, turn the headphones off and on. A headphone firmware update can change behavior - please let us know your firmware version.

Equalizer and spatial audio

Settings → Sound. The equalizer (bass, mid, treble) is saved on the headphones themselves, so it applies on every device. Spatial audio (off / room / head) is available on QuietComfort Ultra models; switching the listening mode - e.g. to Immersion - can change it. If a control is missing, your model doesn't report that feature.

Compatibility

iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 18 or later): tested with Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen). Also supported (beta): QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen), QuietComfort Headphones (2nd Gen), QuietComfort Ultra Headphones and Earbuds (1st Gen). Older models need the Mac app.

Mac (macOS 13 or later): tested with Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen). Also supported (beta): QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen), QuietComfort Headphones (1st and 2nd Gen), QuietComfort 45, QuietComfort Earbuds, QuietComfort 35 / 35 II, Ultra Open Earbuds. Experimental: QuietComfort Ultra Headphones and Earbuds (1st Gen). Modes depend on the model.
